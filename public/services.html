Services

Industrial Automation and Machine-Design Support for Qualifying Companies

Every engagement is scoped as a defined project deliverable for a qualifying manufacturing or industrial/R&D company and work connected with its own products, systems, or services.

EngineeringXYZ is not a California-licensed professional engineering firm and does not offer civil, structural, building/facility engineering, PE-stamped services, or electrical contracting or installation. Organizational eligibility and the proposed scope are reviewed before work begins.

01

Machine and Prototype Design Support

Typical situation: A company-owned machine or equipment concept exists — a sketch, a rough CAD model, or a proof-of-principle — but it needs a defined design package for client and vendor review.

Typical problems: Unclear machine architecture, unresolved motion and actuation choices, missing drawings, incomplete vendor interfaces, or a prototype that has not been validated against agreed production requirements.

Objective: Design documentation prepared for fabrication review against stated requirements, assumptions, and client acceptance criteria.

Discuss a prototype Related project →

Possible Deliverables

  • Concept layouts and mechanical architecture
  • 3D CAD models
  • Drawing documentation for client and vendor review
  • Build-readiness review
  • Support for client-led testing and commissioning

02

Fixture and Workholding Design Support

Typical situation: Operators are positioning parts by feel, a process depends on skill instead of tooling, or an assembly step keeps producing variation nobody can trace.

Typical problems: Inconsistent positioning, slow loading and unloading, operator-to-operator variation, mis-loads that reach downstream stations, and workholding that fights the part.

Objective: Design toward documented positioning, cycle-time, and operator-use requirements, subject to client validation.

Discuss a fixture Related project →

Possible Deliverables

  • Fixture concepts and workholding systems
  • Poka-yoke (mistake-proofing) features
  • Operator-flow improvements
  • Tolerance and interface review
  • 3D CAD and fabrication drawings

03

Internal Inspection and Test-Equipment Design Support

Typical situation: Inspection or test is a bottleneck: parts queue up, results vary by operator, or measurements depend on how the part happens to be staged.

Typical problems: Inspection bottlenecks, non-repeatable part presentation, manual staging, gauge-access problems, and test setups that cannot keep pace with production.

Objective: Design toward project-specific repeatability and throughput targets, with final validation by the client.

Discuss an inspection system Related project →

Possible Deliverables

  • Inspection positioning systems
  • Test-station mechanical design
  • Staging and part-presentation fixtures
  • Integration with existing gauges and optics
  • Engineering documentation

04

CAD and Drawing Documentation for Industrial Equipment

Typical situation: Designs are stuck between concept and release: models exist but drawings do not, tolerances were never analyzed, or a vendor returned a quote full of questions.

Typical problems: Unfinished drawings, undocumented design intent, tolerance stack-ups nobody checked, interfaces that do not quite fit, and drawing backlogs blocking releases.

Objective: Documentation intended to support vendor quotation and fabrication review, with open assumptions and interfaces identified.

Discuss a design package Related project →

Possible Deliverables

  • 3D CAD modeling
  • Drawing documentation (GD&T as appropriate)
  • Tolerance and interface review
  • Design-for-assembly review
  • Drawing-release support

05

Manufacturing-Process and Internal-Equipment Design Support

Typical situation: A production step needs dedicated equipment that does not exist off the shelf — or existing equipment needs modification the OEM will not support.

Typical problems: Manual steps that limit throughput, equipment concepts too expensive as first drafted, and machines specified without regard for maintenance access or operator safety.

Objective: Design support based on documented client-provided constraints such as footprint, operators, utilities, maintenance, and safety interfaces.

Discuss production equipment Related project →

Possible Deliverables

  • Equipment layouts
  • Mechanical architecture and simplification studies
  • 3D CAD and drawing documentation
  • Design-for-assembly review
  • Design packages for client and vendor review

06

Defined Project-Recovery and Design-Capacity Support

Typical situation: Your mechanical backlog exceeds your team, a hire is months away, or a project inherited from a departed engineer or a vendor has stalled mid-stream.

Typical problems: Backlogged mechanical work, drawing-release workload, vendor coordination overhead, half-finished designs with missing context, and deadlines that will not move.

Objective: Defined design and documentation capacity for an agreed industrial scope, without an open-ended staffing commitment.

Discuss capacity support Related project →

Possible Deliverables

  • Design review and troubleshooting
  • Project recovery assessments
  • Overflow design and drawing work
  • Vendor coordination
  • Design documentation and handoff packages

Controls, Installation, and Performance Boundaries

Controls support may include control architecture, PLC/HMI software, interface definitions, and design documentation within the agreed industrial scope. Panel fabrication, field wiring, facility connections, permits, inspections, and electrical installation are performed and accepted by the client's qualified panel shop and appropriately licensed electrical contractor.

Performance figures are project-specific objectives, not warranties, unless expressly stated as written acceptance criteria in a signed agreement. The client remains responsible for final site validation, production release, operation, training, maintenance, and later modifications, subject to the responsibility matrix in the agreement.
