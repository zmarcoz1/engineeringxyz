Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EngineeringXYZ do?
EngineeringXYZ is a mechanical engineering consultancy that takes machine prototypes, fixtures, inspection systems, and production equipment from concept — or from a stalled state — to build-ready engineering: mechanical architecture, 3D CAD, engineering drawings, and vendor handoff.
How small can a first project be?
Most engagements start with a focused first scope: a design review, a concept package, or a single fixture. First scopes typically run two to four weeks. No large initial commitment is required.
Who leads the engineering work?
Every engagement is led directly by Marco Wu, Ph.D. When a project requires additional specialist support, EngineeringXYZ coordinates that work while remaining the client's single technical and project point of contact.
Which industries and clients does EngineeringXYZ serve?
Small and mid-sized manufacturers, hardware and medical-device companies, R&D organizations, production and quality teams, and automation companies that need mechanical engineering support. Based in Irvine, California, serving Southern California on-site and remote clients nationwide.
Can confidential drawings and specifications be shared?
Yes. Confidential project information can be reviewed under NDA. Photos, drawings, and specifications are typically reviewed after initial contact.
