Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EngineeringXYZ do?
EngineeringXYZ provides project-specific industrial automation and machine-design support to manufacturers and industrial R&D teams for work connected with their own products, systems, or services.
How small can a first project be?
Most engagements start with a focused first scope: a design review, a concept package, or a single fixture. First scopes typically run two to four weeks. No large initial commitment is required.
Who leads the project work?
Marco Wu, Ph.D., serves as project lead for each agreed EngineeringXYZ scope. Additional specialists may support defined deliverables, while each client, vendor, installer, and specialist remains responsible for its own work.
Which industries and clients does EngineeringXYZ serve?
EngineeringXYZ considers work for manufacturers, industrial R&D teams, utilities, and other industrial companies when the work is connected with their own products, systems, or services. Eligibility and technical fit are reviewed before an engagement begins.
Can confidential drawings and specifications be shared?
Yes. Confidential project information can be reviewed under NDA. Photos, drawings, and specifications are typically reviewed after initial contact.
