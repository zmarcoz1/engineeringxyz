About

An Engineering Consultancy Built for Practical Problems

EngineeringXYZ is a mechanical engineering consultancy based in Irvine, California — led by an experienced principal engineer, Dr. Marco Wu, and supported by a trusted network of specialized engineering professionals when projects require additional expertise or capacity. The firm takes on the mechanical work that sits between an idea and a machine on the floor: prototypes, fixtures, inspection systems, production equipment, and the engineering documentation that lets vendors build them.

Dr. Wu holds a Ph.D. in mechanical engineering and brings more than ten years of experience across machine prototypes, fixtures, inspection systems, production equipment, automation, engineering management, and project execution.

The work is guided by a simple standard: a design is not done when the CAD looks right — it is done when it can be built, operated, maintained, and handed off. That means designing around manufacturing constraints, operator use, and vendor realities from the first concept layout.

What Working with EngineeringXYZ Looks Like

A single point of accountability

One engineering lead owns the project end to end. When additional disciplines are needed, trusted specialists are coordinated under that same accountability — no contractor finger-pointing.

Direct technical communication

You talk to the engineer leading the work. Questions get technical answers, and problems get raised early rather than smoothed over.

Judgment, not just modeling

CAD is the easy part. The value is in architecture decisions, tolerance calls, and knowing which complexity a design can afford to drop.

Comfortable with messy starting points

Half-finished designs, missing drawings, departed engineers, stalled vendors — most projects arrive incomplete, and the process is built for that.

Built for handoff

Deliverables are made to leave: vendor-ready drawings, documented design intent, and packages your internal team can own after the engagement ends.

Scoped like a business decision

Work is defined in phases with clear deliverables, so you can start small, see results, and decide whether to continue.
